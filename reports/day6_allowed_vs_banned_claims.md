# Day 6 — Allowed vs Banned Claims

The controlled vocabulary for every PYRIN-PLAQUE deliverable (README, demo, cards, reports).

## ✅ Allowed claims (safe language)
- "pyrin-regulatory permissiveness" / "myeloid pyrin-permissiveness"
- "suggestive myeloid localization"
- "directional but confounded bulk triangulation"
- "dimensionless hypothesis-generating ODE"
- "caveat-aware priority score"
- "prioritized for future validation"
- "MEFV/pyrin threshold axis is the top candidate at moderate confidence"
- "NLRP3 is a saturated, well-studied comparator"
- "generic inflammatory priming may dominate pyrin-specific threshold effects"

## ⛔ Banned claims (never use)
- "MEFV causes atherosclerosis" / "MEFV causes plaque instability"
- "validated pyrin target" / "drug target"
- "colchicine proves pyrin mechanism"
- "single-cell expression proves causality" / "proves mechanism"
- "ODE predicts clinical outcomes" / "clinical prediction"
- "first ever" / "first to show"
- "drug recommendation" / "treatment recommendation"
- "patient stratification tool"
- "high-confidence target"

## Mapping rule
Any deliverable that would require a banned phrase to be accurate is **downgraded**, not published. Every claim must trace to a source claim card or a computed artifact (table/figure), and inherit the relevant caveat(s) from reports/day5_caveat_ledger.md.
