## Downgrade summary (before single-cell analysis)
- **No claim requires a confidence downgrade.** All ten are high-confidence, PASS, and source-resolvable.
- **Two WATCH items (scope, not confidence):**
  - **C-A-09** — its readouts (caspase-1, IL-18) are *shared downstream* effectors; supports pyrin *activation* but must never be used as pyrin-vs-NLRP3 discriminative evidence (Day-1 caveat 1).
  - **C-A-04** — PYCARD/ASC is a shared assembly node, not a discriminative feature; keep as shared node only.
- **Scope reminder for all mechanistic claims (C-A-01/02/03/06/07/08):** these establish *mechanism*, not *human plaque cell-state localization*. They justify which genes belong in PYRIN_MODULE; they do NOT pre-judge where pyrin-permissive states sit in GSE159677. Single-cell analysis must test localization independently.
- **Highest-value PI target: C-A-11** (PAPA PSTPIP1 activates pyrin but not NLRP3) — cleanest pyrin/NLRP3 dissociation; verify first.
