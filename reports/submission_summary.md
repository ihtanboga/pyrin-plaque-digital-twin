# PYRIN-PLAQUE Digital Twin — Submission Summary

PYRIN-PLAQUE is an auditable, public-data-only Claude Science workflow that asks whether MEFV/pyrin inflammasome biology converges on specific human atherosclerotic plaque cell states, distinct from the saturated NLRP3 literature. Using MCP connectors (GEO, PubMed, ChEMBL, Open Targets, ClinicalTrials.gov, InterPro), it builds a 57-claim reviewer-gated evidence graph, scores pyrin vs NLRP3 backbones in verified single-cell plaque data (GSE159677), triangulates against bulk stable/unstable plaque (GSE120521), and runs a dimensionless pyroptosis digital twin.

The honest result is suggestive, not definitive: a myeloid pyrin-permissiveness pattern (MEFV sparse but myeloid-enriched; PYRIN_BACKBONE highest in macrophages). Crucially, null and confounded findings were preserved, not hidden — bulk signal collapses under myeloid residualization, the plaque-core signal is mixed, and the ODE shows generic priming dominating the pyrin threshold. A caveat-aware priority score ranks the MEFV/pyrin threshold axis top at only moderate confidence; nothing reaches high confidence. Six adversarial reviewer-agent gates enforce conservative claims throughout. It is an auditable hypothesis engine, not a validated-target or clinical claim.
