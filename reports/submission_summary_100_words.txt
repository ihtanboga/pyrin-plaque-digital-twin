PYRIN-PLAQUE is an auditable, public-data-only Claude Science workflow asking whether MEFV/pyrin biology maps to specific atherosclerotic plaque cell states, distinct from saturated NLRP3 literature. Using MCP connectors, it builds a reviewer-gated evidence graph, scores pyrin vs NLRP3 backbones in single-cell plaque data (GSE159677), triangulates against bulk plaque (GSE120521), and runs a dimensionless pyroptosis digital twin. The result is a suggestive myeloid pyrin-permissiveness pattern. Null and confounded findings were preserved, not hidden: bulk signal is myeloid-confounded and generic priming dominates the pyrin threshold. Six reviewer-agent gates enforce conservative claims. An auditable hypothesis engine, not a validated-target or clinical claim.
