PYRIN-PLAQUE is an auditable, public-data-only Claude Science workflow asking whether MEFV/pyrin inflammasome biology maps to specific human atherosclerotic plaque cell states, distinct from the saturated NLRP3 literature. Via MCP connectors (GEO, PubMed, ChEMBL, Open Targets, ClinicalTrials.gov, InterPro), it builds a 57-claim reviewer-gated evidence graph, scores pyrin vs NLRP3 backbones in verified single-cell plaque data (GSE159677), triangulates against bulk plaque (GSE120521), and runs a dimensionless pyroptosis digital twin. The result is suggestive: a myeloid pyrin-permissiveness pattern, with MEFV sparse but myeloid-enriched. Null and confounded findings were preserved, not hidden — bulk signal collapses under myeloid residualization, the core signal is mixed, and the ODE shows generic priming dominating the pyrin threshold. A caveat-aware priority score ranks the MEFV/pyrin threshold axis top at moderate confidence only. Six reviewer-agent gates enforce conservative claims. It is an auditable hypothesis engine, not a validated-target or clinical claim.
